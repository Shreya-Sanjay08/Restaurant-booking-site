Our Story

Born from the Souk.
Inspired by the World.

Long before the skyscrapers rose, Sharjah's souks were where the world met. Spice traders from India, pearl merchants from Persia, fishermen from the coast — every culture left its mark on the city's flavour. Souk Kitchen carries that tradition into the dining room.

Our menu is an honest reflection of the UAE today: a table where ouzi lamb and truffle hummus sit beside saffron risotto and Filipino adobo tacos. Not fusion for its own sake — but food that honours the stories of the 200+ nationalities who call this country home. Every dish here carries a memory. Come share yours.

Signature Dishes

Stories Worth Tasting

01

Ouzi Lamb Shoulder

Slow-roasted · 12 hours · Heritage Recipe

The ouzi is perhaps the UAE's most generous dish — a whole lamb, slow-cooked underground, served on a mountain of fragrant rice for celebrations. Our version honours that spirit: a whole shoulder, 12 hours in the oven, bathed in saffron and aromatic spices until the meat surrenders to the bone. Served on Emirati-spiced rice with toasted almonds, pine nuts and a cool rose water yoghurt. A dish that was built for sharing.

AED 185 per person

02

Truffle Hummus

Palestinian recipe · Black Truffle · Warm Khubz

A Palestinian grandmother's hummus recipe — chickpeas soaked overnight, peeled by hand, blended with tahini from Nablus. Then something unexpected: black truffle shavings from Périgord. Two worlds that shouldn't meet but absolutely do. Served with warm khubz pulled from our clay oven and cold-pressed Palestinian olive oil. The dish that converts sceptics into believers.

AED 65 to share
Reserve & Request This Dish
03

Saffron Crème Brûlée

Emirati Saffron · Cardamom · Caramelised Crust

Saffron has been traded through the souks of the Gulf for centuries — its warmth wound into everything from rice to ghawa coffee. Here, it meets a French classic: a silken custard steeped overnight with the finest Emirati saffron and green cardamom, finished tableside with a crackling caramelised sugar crust. A dessert that ends the meal exactly as it should — with a moment of quiet pleasure.

AED 55 per serve
